Jag & Simran meet Randy and Neetu at arrivals (Calgary to SFO), then we point the car north into the city.

Stop 1

Painted Ladies

The famous row of Victorian homes on Steiner St, facing Alamo Square Park. It’s the closest stop to the airport, so we hit it first. Postcard shot with the skyline behind us.

Group photoAlamo Square
Stop 2

Palace of Fine Arts

A Roman-style rotunda reflected in a lagoon, built for the 1915 Panama-Pacific Expo. Swans, columns, and the best golden-hour backdrop in the city.

Golden hourMarina District
Stop 3

Marina walk

Stroll the Marina Green and bayfront toward Crissy Field. Sailboats, joggers, and the bridge glowing in the distance.

Stop 4

Golden Gate Bridge

The icon. Walk out on the Crissy Field side, or take the Welcome Center view for the full International Orange moment. If fog rolls in, that’s the vibe.
